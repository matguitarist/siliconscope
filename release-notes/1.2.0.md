- Update Intel apps right from the list: an Update button next to every app with an Apple Silicon version or an update, and Update All… for all of them at once
- Homebrew apps install with brew; apps with their own update feed download, are checked against the developer's signatures, and replace the old copy, which goes to the Trash
- App Store apps open their store page
- When Homebrew needs your password, Finish Update in Terminal does the rest
- After an update, the list and the log show what the app is now: Universal or Apple Silicon
